## Interpretation and remaining architecture work

Twelve *scheduled* retries after initial parking aligns the stated two-hour cadence with about 24 hours; counting the original failed submission would normally end near 22 hours. Architect confirmed this interpretation in A: Q-analyst-1; it is not a new owner quote. Waiting time and processing delays make 24 hours approximate; JOB_RETENTION is not a queue TTL.

An atomic local file does not transact with Telegram. Ordinary restart durability must not be described as exactly-once external delivery across a crash at publication. Architect confirmed fixed delay plus a full-interval initial delay (restart restarts the wait), a pass guard, and fallback to ordinary failure if initial parking cannot be persisted. See [api](api.md), [flows](flows.md) and [questions](comms/questions.md). Functional steps must use scheduled time/short real schedules; direct runner calls belong to unit tests.
